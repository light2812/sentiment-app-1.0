This project focuses on performing sentiment analysis on hotel reviews
within the travel and tourism sector. Utilizing Python and libraries such as
`pandas`, `seaborn`, and maybe `Streamlit`, the project classifies reviews into
positive, negative, or neutral sentiments. The project conducts a thorough
analysis of customer feedback to classify reviews into positive, negative, or
neutral sentiments. The analysis includes examining sentiment distributions,
identifying common themes, and summarizing key insights from the data.
The primary goal is to provide a detailed understanding of guest sentiments,
uncovering trends and patterns that can inform hotel management decisions
and enhance the quality of service, possibly using an interactive web
application. By providing a comprehensive view of customer feedback, the
application aims to assist hotel management in understanding guest
experiences and identifying key areas for improvement, ultimately enhancing
decision-making and service quality in the hospitality industry.
